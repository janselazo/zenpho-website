How buyers choose software

They compare. Then they sign up.

Buyers rarely subscribe from the first ad they see. They watch a demo, read reviews, start a trial, then decide who feels credible. You need to win the discovery scroll, the high-intent search, and the follow-up that activates the account.

01The discovery moment

Product reels, founder video, and motion creative on Meta and LinkedIn build awareness and feed your retargeting pool. Reach only pays off when the funnel behind it converts.

02The intent moment

Branded, competitor, and category search capture buyers ready to compare. Show up with sharp landing pages, social proof, and a clear path to trial or demo.

Get tracking and pages right first. Add search for buyers comparing alternatives. Use social video to feed the top of funnel, then close with onboarding and sales follow-up.

Invest first
  • Paid social

  • Search & retargeting

  • Trial & demo follow-up

Compound over time
  • Own the product story

  • Unit economics, not vanity

    Track CAC, activation, and payback together so growth stays profitable instead of buying signups you cannot convert.

The problems

Why trials go cold

Most SaaS companies do not have a traffic problem. They have a system problem.

Creative that does not explain the product

Feature screenshots and stock UI do not sell outcomes. Without demo video and founder-led creative, CPMs climb and CAC rises while activation stays flat.

Weak creative is a top driver of rising SaaS CAC

Trials that never activate

Most free trials stall before the aha moment. Without onboarding nudges and sales follow-up, you pay to acquire users who never become customers.

Low activation is the silent killer of SaaS unit economics

Demos stuck in the inbox

Inbound demo requests sit too long while buyers evaluate competitors. Slow response turns hot intent into a lost deal.

Speed-to-lead still wins in B2B software sales

Broken tracking, blind scaling

Missing events and messy attribution make platforms guess. Bad data means you scale the wrong campaigns and cut the winners.

Weak tracking quietly distorts most ad-account decisions

Burned by agencies before

Long contracts, vanity metrics, and reports full of impressions instead of CAC and activated trials. You want real numbers on a live dashboard.

Most founders report minimal ROI from past ad retainers
