The challenge

What you are dealing with

Most startup delays are not engineering problems. They are scope, clarity, and accountability problems wearing a technical disguise.

No technical team yet

A product idea stuck waiting on hires you cannot afford, or freelancers who disappear mid-build.

Average agency quote: 4 to 6 months before first demo

Timeline pressure

Investors, accelerators, or early customers expect traction while the product is still a slide deck.

Founder-blind development

Developers who ship features but never think about runway, activation, or what proves the idea.

Weak launch surface

A landing page that does not explain the product, capture interest, or convert early signups.

Rebuild risk

Architecture choices that work for a demo but collapse when your first hundred users arrive.

How we work

Built by someone who has shipped

Zenpho is led by an engineer who founded and launched Taptok to more than fifteen thousand customers. That operator lens shapes every scope decision.

We do not hand you a roadmap and vanish. We scope the smallest coherent version, build it in about two weeks, and leave you with software you can demo, instrument, and iterate.

Design, engineering, and launch stay under one roof — so your MVP, landing page, and production deploy are not three separate vendors.
