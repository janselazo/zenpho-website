How patients choose

They compare. Then they call.

New patients rarely book the first practice they see. They search symptoms and prices, scan reviews, watch a short video, then decide who feels credible. You need to show up in both moments: when they are researching and when they are ready to book.

01The research moment

Emergency visits, Invisalign quotes, and implant comparisons start on Google and Maps. Show up with accurate listings, a credible site, and search campaigns tied to real offers.

02The trust moment

Before anyone calls, they scroll. Provider video, treatment explainers, and proof you are a real team lower the hesitation that keeps chairs empty.

Align your listings and website first. Add paid search for patients ready to book. Use social video so strangers feel familiar before the first call.

Invest first
  • Paid search

    Capture high-intent local queries with one clear offer per campaign, a matching landing page, and tracking on calls and form fills.

  • Paid social

    Use short video and promo creative to stay visible between need moments. Reach is cheap; booked patients only follow when follow-up is fast.

  • Every lead into CRM

    No inquiry should live in a voicemail or DMs folder. Capture contact info, alert staff instantly, and nurture unbooked treatment over time.

Compound over time
  • Own local discovery

    Google Business Profile, Bing Places, and Apple Maps should stay current with photos, hours, and weekly updates that signal an active practice.

  • Website matches reality

    Services, hours, and offers on your site should mirror your listings. Search engines and AI assistants cross-check both before recommending you.

The problems

Why chairs sit empty

Most practices do not have a marketing problem. They have a system problem.

Referrals are not a plan

Word of mouth built your practice, but it does not fill open chairs on demand. When the schedule softens, last-minute ad spend rarely closes the gap. You need a repeatable way to bring in new patients every month.

35% of dentists reported not being busy enough in Q3 2025

Ads that look like every other dentist

Stock photos and generic copy do not build trust. Real video and before/after creative routinely outperform templated creative by 30 to 50 percent.

Weak creative is the #2 reason dental Facebook ads fail

Leads leaking out the bottom

Missed calls, slow follow-up, and no-shows drain ROI faster than bad ad copy. You can run flawless ads into a bucket with a hole in it.

Missed calls alone can cost $80k–$120k per year

Diagnosed treatment never booked

Nearly half of treatment plans go unscheduled. Patients are not rejecting care, they are rejecting uncertainty. Follow up within 48 hours with clear next steps.

Average case acceptance sits around 50–60%

Burned by agencies before

Long contracts, vague deliverables, and vanity metrics instead of cost per booked patient. You want a real number on a live dashboard, not engagement up 42 percent.

73% report minimal ROI from past social campaigns
