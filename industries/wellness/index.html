How clients choose

They explore. Then they commit.

Wellness clients rarely buy from one Instagram ad. They compare programs, read reviews, watch provider content, then decide who feels credible and worth the visit. Win the discovery scroll and the intent-ready search.

01The discovery moment

Weight loss, hormone care, and wellness center searches happen on Google and Maps. Show up with sharp listings, program pages, and search ads tied to one offer at a time.

02The lifestyle moment

Social is where aspiration builds. Program reels, provider intros, and membership creative keep your brand top of mind before someone is ready to book.

Get listings and site aligned first. Run search for ready-to-book demand. Use social video and promos so your name is familiar when they click Book.

Invest first
  • Paid search

    Target program and location keywords with dedicated landing pages, call tracking, and instant alerts when a discovery request comes in.

  • Paid social

    Membership and program promos on Instagram and Facebook drive discovery. Booked sessions follow when creative feels native and your team responds fast.

  • Every lead into CRM

    DMs, forms, and missed calls should land in one pipeline. Capture contacts, automate follow-up, and reactivate lapsed members over time.

Compound over time
  • Own local discovery

    Google Business Profile, Bing, and Apple Maps need fresh photos, offers, and weekly posts so you look active in local results.

  • Website matches reality

    Program menus, pricing context, and hours on your site should match your listings. Search and AI tools validate both before surfacing you.

The problems

Why growth stalls

Most wellness brands do not need more posts. They need a system that turns attention into booked discovery calls and members.

Inconsistent booking flow

Promo spikes fill the calendar, then quiet weeks follow. Without a repeatable acquisition system, you keep restarting from zero every season.

Empty calendar blocks are the #1 growth bottleneck

Creative that blends in

Stock wellness imagery and generic offer copy look like every center in the feed. Authentic provider video consistently outperforms templates.

Video drives the best engagement but is underused

Paid leads that never book

Slow response, missed DMs, and no follow-up on form fills waste ad spend. Speed to lead and persistent nurture beat bigger budgets.

Most discovery requests need follow-up within 5 minutes

Trials that never convert

Clients try one session but never join a membership or package. Automated nurture and clear next steps recover revenue you already earned.

Unconverted trials are margin left on the table

Agency fatigue

Retainers with vague deliverables and reports full of impressions. You want cost per booked session on a dashboard you can read.

Most owners cannot name their true cost per acquisition

Yes. We typically pair Meta for discovery with Google for high-intent local search, unified in one CRM pipeline.

Yes. Landing pages, nurture sequences, and reporting can be scoped around memberships, trials, or single-program offers.
