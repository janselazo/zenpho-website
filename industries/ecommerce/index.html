How shoppers buy

They discover. Then they convert.

Shoppers rarely buy from the first ad they see. They scroll, compare prices and reviews, abandon a cart, then come back when the offer and timing feel right. You need to win the discovery scroll, the high-intent search, and the follow-up that closes the sale.

01The discovery moment

Product reels, UGC, and motion creative on Meta and TikTok build desire and feed your retargeting pool. Cheap reach only pays off when the funnel behind it converts.

02The intent moment

Shopping, Performance Max, and branded search capture buyers ready to add to cart. Show up with sharp feeds, fast pages, and offers tied to your best-sellers.

The problems

Why carts stay empty

Most stores do not have a traffic problem. They have a system problem.

Creative that stops working

Winning ads fatigue fast. Without a steady pipeline of fresh product video and UGC, CPMs climb and ROAS slides while you scramble for the next hook.

Creative fatigue is the #1 driver of rising ecommerce CPA

Abandoned carts everywhere

Most shoppers add to cart and leave. Without automated cart and browse recovery, you pay to acquire buyers and then let them slip away.

~70% of online carts are abandoned on average

One-and-done buyers

Acquisition keeps getting more expensive, so profit lives in repeat purchases. Stores with no retention flows leave most lifetime value on the table.

Repeat customers can drive 40%+ of revenue when nurtured

Broken tracking, blind spending

iOS changes and missing server-side events make platforms guess. Bad data means you scale the wrong ads and cut the winners.

Weak tracking quietly distorts most ad-account decisions

Burned by agencies before

Long contracts, vanity metrics, and reports full of impressions instead of ROAS and contribution margin. You want real numbers on a live dashboard.

Most brands report minimal ROI from past ad retainers
