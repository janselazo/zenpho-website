How it works

Reviews FAQ

Common questions.

№ I When do review requests go out?

You configure timing after a completed appointment or visit — typically a few hours later so the experience is fresh but not rushed.

№ II Can we choose which platform to ask for?

Yes. Most local businesses prioritize Google, but you can point clients to the review site that matters most for your market.

№ III Does this work with appointments and leads?

Yes. Review and referral automations tie to client records created from booking, pipeline, or intake — one system, not another login.

№ IV Is this included in the growth packages?

Review and referral flows are part of full-funnel setups and can be added to existing platform accounts. Book a call to map what you need.
