Before you brief us

Questions, answered.

№ I How fast can you turn around a video?

Your first cut lands in 48 hours once the brief is signed off. Folios and retainers run on a weekly production cadence so there's always fresh creative for the algorithm.

№ II Do I own the videos outright?

Yes. All footage, edits, audio licensing and source files are handed over with full perpetual usage rights for paid media, organic and your site — including AI-generated UGC.

№ III Which aspect ratios do you deliver?

Every video ships in 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, Stories) and 1:1 (feed). Need 16:9 for YouTube? It's included at no extra cost.

№ IV Can I mix Motion, UGC and Claymation?

Absolutely — and we recommend it. We'll run the formats against each other in your ad account, scale the winners and kill the losers. Most brands land on a blend within the first month.

№ V Do you handle the media buying too?

Yes. Once your videos are ready, we can run your paid campaigns on Meta and TikTok — setup, testing, and optimization — as a follow-on service. Start with the creatives, then plug us in when you're ready to scale.
