Frequently asked

Common questions.

№ I How long to launch a mobile app?

Two to four weeks for a focused MVP — design in week one, build in weeks two and three, TestFlight and store submission in week four. Apple review usually runs 24–48 hours.

№ II Do you build iOS only, Android only, or both?

Both, by default — one React Native codebase, two native apps. We can ship iOS first if you want to validate before paying the Play Console fee.

№ III Do I need Apple and Google developer accounts?

Yes — Apple ($99/yr) and Google Play ($25 one-time) accounts must be in your name. We help you set them up and guide store review.

№ IV Do I keep ownership of the code?

Yes. Code lives in your GitHub from day one. We commit, you own. We can transfer repos at any point with no exit fee.

№ V Will users be able to pay in the app?

Yes — RevenueCat for in-app purchases and subscriptions on iOS and Android, plus trials and entitlements as scoped.

№ VI Push notifications and deep links?

Yes. FCM / APNs, deep links, and basic retention messages are part of every mobile build.

№ VII Who maintains the app after launch?

We can stay on a fractional-CTO retainer or hand over to your engineer with architecture docs and post-launch support included in the package.
