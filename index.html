FAQ

Common questions

№ I What do you do?

We design, build, and launch digital products — websites, online stores, and custom web and mobile apps. One product studio handles design and engineering together, so everything ships fast and works as one system.

№ II Who is Zenpho for?

Founders building e-commerce brands and tech products. If you have an idea that needs building, a store that needs to convert, or a product that needs to ship, and you want a technical partner who has done it before, you are who this is built for. We work in English and Spanish.

№ III Are you an agency or a dev shop?

We are a product studio — closer to a senior engineering partner than a generic agency or freelance bench. Zenpho is led by an engineer and founder who scopes, builds, and launches real products, so design and code come from one team that understands your business.

№ IV What services do you offer?

Four build services: custom websites and landing pages, e-commerce stores, web apps, and mobile apps. Start with what you need most — each engagement follows the same Design · Build · Launch cadence.

№ V How fast can you ship?

Fast. We build MVPs and launch-ready sites and stores in weeks, not months, because speed is what a founder actually needs. We give you a clear timeline before we start and keep you seeing progress the whole way.

№ VI Can you build my MVP or product from scratch?

Yes. That is core to what we do. We take an idea, decide what to build first, design it, and engineer a working product you can put in front of real users — launch-ready from version one.

№ VII Do you handle launch and deployment?

Yes. Every project includes staging, QA, production deployment, analytics wired in, and handoff documentation. We do not disappear after the Figma file — we ship something you can put in customers' hands.

№ VIII I have worked with an agency before and it did not work. How are you different?

Most vendors hand you a deck or a theme and vanish before launch. We scope the smallest coherent version, build it ourselves, and stay through deployment. The person doing the work is an engineer who has shipped and launched real products before. You get one team accountable for the result, not three vendors pointing fingers.
